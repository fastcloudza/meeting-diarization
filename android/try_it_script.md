# A made up meeting to read aloud

This is a script for trying the recorder. The business, the people and the order
are invented. Two people read it, one line each in turn, at a normal speaking pace,
sitting where one phone can hear both of them. Dana speaks first, because the
method treats the first person heard as the agent. It takes about two minutes.

The business is Harbour Light Bakery, a small bakery that takes catering orders.
Dana runs the counter. Tomas is a customer who wants to order for his office.

**Dana:** Good morning, Tomas, thanks for coming in. I'm Dana, I run the counter
here at Harbour Light Bakery. You said on the phone it's about catering?

**Tomas:** Yes, good morning. We have a team lunch on the twelfth of next month,
and I'd like to order pastries and sandwiches for about thirty people.

**Dana:** Lovely. Thirty is a good size for us. Do you want a mix, or one kind of
sandwich for everybody?

**Tomas:** A mix, I think. Some vegetarian, because four of us don't eat meat, and
one colleague can't have gluten.

**Dana:** Okay, four vegetarian and one gluten free. Our gluten free loaf is baked
on Thursdays, so for the twelfth we would bake it the day before and keep it
separate from everything else.

**Tomas:** That would be perfect. What would you suggest for the pastries?

**Dana:** Most offices like the almond croissants and the cinnamon rolls. I would
do twenty croissants and twenty rolls, so there are a few left over.

**Tomas:** Forty is fine. What does that come to, with the sandwiches?

**Dana:** Sandwiches are fifty five rand each box of four, so eight boxes is four
hundred and forty. Croissants and rolls are two hundred and eighty together. That
makes seven hundred and twenty rand, plus delivery.

**Tomas:** And delivery to Bree Street?

**Dana:** Bree Street is thirty rand. So seven hundred and fifty rand in total.

**Tomas:** That's reasonable. Can you deliver by half past eleven? We eat at noon.

**Dana:** Half past eleven works. I'll put you down for the twelfth, delivery at
eleven thirty, and I'll send a confirmation to your email today.

**Tomas:** Thank you, Dana. My email is tomas at northgate dot example.

**Dana:** Got it. We will see you on the twelfth. Have a good day, Tomas.

**Tomas:** You too, goodbye.
